About This Project

This tool supports the identification of boreholes at risk of contamination from nearby sanitation infrastructure across urban Malawi. Using satellite-derived building footprints, each borehole is assessed across two independent proximity zones — a 30 m immediate setback zone (direct wellhead hazard) and a 100 m neighbourhood zone (cumulative loading). Each zone is assigned its own risk tier from P1 (Critical) to P5 (Baseline) to triage where ground-truthing and water quality testing are most urgently needed.

Risk Classification Guide
The 30 m and 100 m zones represent two independent contamination mechanisms and are rated separately. Dot fill = 30 m tier. Outer ring colour = 100 m tier.

● Inner Zone — 30 m Setback (~0.28 ha) | Immediate Hazard

Evaluates wellhead breakthrough risk and 30-day pathogen die-off failure. Dot fill colour reflects this rating.

30 m Tier (dot fill colour)
P1 — Critical
Direct wellhead breakthrough; latrines packed within travel distance
P2 — High
High plume proximity under wet-season water table mounding
P3 — Moderate
Manageable risk; standard lateral setbacks maintained
P4 — Low
Isolated discharges contained in shallow saprolite
P5 — Baseline
Pristine sanitary zone

○ Outer Zone — 100 m Neighbourhood (~3.14 ha) | Cumulative Loading

Evaluates total assimilative breakdown, plume overlap, and dilution capacity. Outer ring colour reflects this rating.

100 m Tier (outer ring colour)
P1 — Critical
Total assimilative breakdown; effluent overwhelms natural dilution recharge
P2 — High
Plume overlap; effluent represents significant fraction of recharge
P3 — Moderate
Semi-contained corridors; effluent partial fraction of recharge
P4 — Low
High dilution capacity; recharge dominant
P5 — Baseline
Ambient groundwater / pristine rural setting
Special Indicators
Yellow outer ring — Dual P1 Alert
Both 30 m and 100 m zones rated P1 Critical. Highest priority for immediate field verification and water quality testing.
