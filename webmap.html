About This Project

This tool supports the identification of boreholes at risk of contamination from nearby sanitation infrastructure across urban Malawi. Using satellite-derived building footprints, each borehole is assessed across two proximity zones - a 30m immediate setback zone and a 100m neighbourhood zone - and assigned a risk tier from P1 (Critical) to P5 (Baseline). The goal is to triage where ground-truthing and water quality testing are most urgently needed, making field efforts targeted and efficient. This dataset will also be used to train a machine or deep learning model to predict water quality risk from satellite data alone.
