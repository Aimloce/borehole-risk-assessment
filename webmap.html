About This Project

This tool supports the identification of boreholes at risk of contamination from nearby sanitation infrastructure across urban Malawi. Using satellite-derived building footprints, each borehole is assessed across two independent proximity zones: a 30 m immediate setback zone (wellhead breakthrough risk) and a 100 m neighbourhood zone (cumulative loading risk). Each zone is rated separately from P1 (Critical) to P5 (Baseline). The goal is to triage where ground-truthing and water quality testing are most urgently needed. This dataset will also be used to train a machine or deep learning model to predict water quality risk from satellite data alone.
